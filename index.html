Fly to Lukla & Trek to Phakding飛往盧卡拉 & 徒步至 Phakding 帕克丁

📍 2,652 m / 8,699 ft 海拔🥾 8 km⏱ 30 min flight + 3 hr trek

Take an early morning flight to Lukla, enjoying spectacular aerial views of the Himalayan range. After landing, meet the trekking crew and begin the trek toward Phakding. The trail follows the Dudh Koshi River through pine forests and traditional Sherpa settlements, crossing suspension bridges and passing Buddhist prayer wheels and mani stones.
清晨搭乘航班飛往盧卡拉，沿途欣賞壯麗的喜馬拉雅山脈空中景色。抵達後與登山團隊會合，開始徒步前往 Phakding。沿途順著 Dudh Koshi 河谷前進，穿越松林與傳統夏爾巴村落，經過吊橋、佛教轉經筒與瑪尼石。

📍 3,440 m / 11,286 ft 海拔🥾 10–12 km⏱ 7 hr

Follow the Dudh Koshi Valley toward Namche Bazaar. Cross several suspension bridges and pass through pine and rhododendron forests. After entering Sagarmatha National Park, cross the famous Hillary Suspension Bridge and begin the steep final ascent toward Namche Bazaar.
沿著 Dudh Koshi 河谷前往 Namche Bazaar。途中穿越多座吊橋與松樹、杜鵑花森林。進入薩加瑪塔國家公園後，跨越著名的 Hillary Suspension Bridge，開始最後一段陡峭上坡，抵達 Namche Bazaar。

Namche Bazaar to TengbocheNamche Bazaar 南池市場 → Tengboche Tengboche

📍 3,860 m / 12,660 ft 海拔🥾 10 km⏱ 5–6 hr

Leave Namche and follow a scenic mountainside trail with magnificent views of Everest, Lhotse, Ama Dablam and Thamserku. The trail descends toward the Dudh Koshi before climbing through dense rhododendron and pine forests to Tengboche, home to the renowned Tengboche Monastery.
離開 Namche，沿著山腰景觀步道前進，可欣賞 Everest、Lhotse、Ama Dablam 與 Thamserku 等壯麗山峰。步道先下降至 Dudh Koshi 河，再穿越茂密的杜鵑花與松林上行至 Tengboche，這裡也是著名 Tengboche Monastery（天波切寺）的所在地。

Tengboche to DingbocheTengboche → Dingboche 丁波切

📍 4,410 m / 14,468 ft 海拔🥾 11 km⏱ 5–6 hr

Descend through the forest to Deboche and cross the Imja Khola. The trail climbs gradually through Pangboche, one of the oldest Sherpa settlements in the region. As you gain altitude, the landscape becomes more open and alpine, with spectacular views of Ama Dablam and the surrounding peaks.
穿過森林下降至 Deboche，並跨越 Imja Khola。之後經過 Pangboche，這是當地歷史悠久的夏爾巴聚落之一。隨著海拔逐漸升高，景觀變得更加開闊且具有高山特色，可以欣賞 Ama Dablam 及周圍群峰的壯麗景色。

Acclimatization Day in DingbocheDingboche 丁波切適應海拔日

📍 4,410 m / 14,468 ft 海拔🥾 5–6 km⏱ 3–4 hr

Dedicated to acclimatization before heading toward Island Peak. Take a gradual hike into the surrounding hills, gaining additional elevation before returning to Dingboche. The hike provides excellent views of Ama Dablam, Lhotse, Nuptse and the surrounding Himalayan landscape. The remainder of the day is for rest and climbing preparation.
今天專門用於適應海拔，為前往 Island Peak 做準備。進行一段循序漸進的周邊山坡健行，增加海拔後返回 Dingboche。途中可欣賞 Ama Dablam、Lhotse、Nuptse 及周圍喜馬拉雅景觀。其餘時間休息並準備接下來的攀登。

Dingboche to Chhukung & Climbing TrainingDingboche 丁波切 → Chhukung 琼孔 & 攀登訓練

📍 4,730 m / 15,518 ft 海拔🥾 6 km⏱ 3–4 hr + training

Trek gradually upward through the Imja Valley toward Chhukung, with spectacular views of Island Peak, Lhotse, Nuptse and Ama Dablam. After reaching Chhukung, the climbing guide conducts essential technical training for the Island Peak ascent.
沿 Imja Valley 緩慢上升前往 Chhukung，可欣賞 Island Peak、Lhotse、Nuptse 與 Ama Dablam 等山峰。抵達 Chhukung 後，由攀登嚮導進行 Island Peak 登頂所需的基本技術訓練。

Training includes: crampon use, ice-axe techniques, rope handling, ascending and descending fixed ropes, and basic climbing safety.

Chhukung to Island Peak Base Camp & Final TrainingChhukung 琼孔 → Island Peak Base Camp 島峰基地營 & 最終訓練

📍 5,240 m / 17,192 ft 海拔🥾 8 km⏱ 4–5 hr + training

Begin the approach toward Island Peak Base Camp. The trail crosses rugged alpine terrain and glacial moraines beneath Island Peak. At Base Camp, settle into camp and conduct final climbing preparations.
開始前往 Island Peak Base Camp。步道穿越崎嶇的高山地形與冰川堆積地形，沿途位於 Island Peak 山腳下。抵達基地營後安頓並進行最後的攀登準備。

The guide reviews the summit route, climbing techniques, equipment, rope systems and safety procedures before the summit attempt.
登頂前，嚮導將再次確認登頂路線、攀登技術、裝備、繩索系統與安全程序。

Summit Island Peak → Chhukung → Helicopter to LuklaIsland Peak 島峰登頂 → Chhukung → 搭直升機前往 Lukla 盧卡拉

🏔 Summit 6,189 m / 20,305 ft 峰頂海拔🥾 Climbing distance 14–16 km⏱ 10–12 hr + helicopter

Begin the summit attempt very early, usually before sunrise. The climb initially follows rocky terrain before reaching the glacier and steeper snow and ice sections. Using crampons, ropes, ice axes and other mountaineering equipment, gradually ascend toward the summit ridge.
登頂日將在非常早的時間出發，通常於日出前開始。初段沿岩石地形攀登，之後進入冰川以及更陡峭的雪地與冰壁區域。使用冰爪、繩索、冰斧及其他高山攀登裝備，逐步向山頂稜線上升。

After reaching the summit of Island Peak at 6,189 meters, enjoy views of Lhotse, Nuptse, Ama Dablam, Makalu, Baruntse and surrounding Himalayan peaks. Carefully descend to Base Camp and continue toward Chhukung.
成功抵達 6,189 米的 Island Peak 峰頂後，可欣賞 Lhotse、Nuptse、Ama Dablam、Makalu、Baruntse 以及周圍眾多喜馬拉雅山峰。之後小心下降至基地營，再繼續前往 Chhukung。

Helicopter transfer: A chartered helicopter from Chhukung to Lukla is planned, subject to weather, visibility, helicopter availability and operational conditions. If conditions prevent the flight, an alternative arrangement may be required.
